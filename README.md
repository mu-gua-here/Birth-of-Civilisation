# Birth of Civilisation
Manage your civilisation well. Balance power, approval, economy, and the social well-being of your subjects.

## About
Start from scratch. Explore the Seven Continents all with their unique attributes, and settle at your preferred location. Compete with other civilisations as you race to be the most powerful and advanced civlisation on the map. Engage in combat and imperialism where desired. Ultimately, embark on a journey towards a strong civlisation.
